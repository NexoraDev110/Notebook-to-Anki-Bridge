# Chrome Web Store listing draft

## Name
Notebook to Anki Bridge

## Short description
Capture Google Notebook flashcards and export them to Anki.

## Detailed description
Notebook to Anki Bridge extracts structured flashcards from supported Google Notebook views and helps you move them into Anki.

Features:
- Capture flashcards from supported fullscreen and embedded/right-side views.
- Preview captured Front and Back text.
- Export cards as CSV or TSV.
- Send all captured cards to an existing Anki deck through AnkiConnect.
- Skip existing/non-addable notes and repeated cards in the current batch.
- Keep your existing Anki deck hierarchy; the extension does not create decks automatically.

Privacy:
- Flashcard response data is processed locally in your browser.
- The extension does not send Notebook content to a developer-operated server.
- Anki import is sent to AnkiConnect on your own computer, only when you click the import action.
- No analytics, advertising, or tracking is integrated.
- See the linked Privacy Policy for full details.

Requirements and limitations:
- Chrome 111 or later. The current Chrome build is 0.3.5.
- AnkiConnect and Anki running locally for direct Anki import.
- Supports the observed Google Notebook RPC formats only; Google may change these undocumented formats.
- Images are not downloaded or embedded; available image alt text may be extracted.

## Single purpose
Capture flashcards from supported Google Notebook pages and export them to CSV/TSV or to the user's local Anki installation.
